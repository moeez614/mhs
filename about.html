About Us

Moeez Ali

Owner of MHS

Moeez Ali is the owner of MHS, currently serving at the MHS,Islamabad. He holds a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BS CS.) degree and has been part of the education department since 2016. In his role as an Moeez High School (MHS) for Science and Computer, Moeez is dedicated to fostering a supportive and engaging learning environment for his students.

Zubair Riaz

Principle of MHS

HOD of Islamiat

Zubair Riaz is a Pakistani-Saudi content creator, historian, and educator renowned for his engaging storytelling on Islamic history and halal travel. Born on January 1, 1992, in Saudi Arabia, he holds dual nationality and has resided in Madinah for over 25 years. His career began as a Quran teacher at Masjid Al-Nabawi, where he taught for more than seven years, contributing to his 15 years of teaching experience in Madinah. In 2021, he transitioned to digital content creation, launching a YouTube channel that now boasts over 1.75 million subscribers and more than 230 million total video views. Through his videos, Zubair takes his audience on spiritual journeys, exploring historical landmarks of Islam, narrating their religious and cultural significance, and sharing halal travel experiences. His popular series include "Hijrat Ka Safar," "Darul Hijra Series," "Hajj Ka Safar," and "Badr Ka Safar," which delve into pivotal events and sacred routes in Islamic history

Dhuruv Rathee

HOD of History

Dhruv Rathee is an Indian YouTuber, vlogger, and social media activist known for his in-depth analyses of political, social, and environmental issues. Born on October 8, 1994, in Rohtak, Haryana, he pursued mechanical engineering and later a master's in renewable energy at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology in Germany. Currently based in Berlin, Rathee has built a substantial online presence, with over 34 million subscribers and 6 billion views across his YouTube channels as of January 2025.

Abrar Hassan

HOD of Tourism

Abrar Hassan is a Pakistani-German travel vlogger and adventurer, widely recognized for his YouTube channel, WildLens by Abrar, where he documents his extensive motorcycle journeys across Asia, Europe, Africa, and the Middle East. Born in Nankana Sahib, Punjab, Pakistan, he pursued aerospace engineering at the Institute of Space Technology in Islamabad and later completed a master's degree in computational mechanics in Germany. After over a decade working in the automotive industry, Abrar transitioned to full-time travel vlogging in 2019. Since then, he has traveled to more than 90 countries, covering at least 24 of them on his motorcycle.
