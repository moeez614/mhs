Events

Events

The school funfair was a much-anticipated event that brought excitement and cheer to students, parents, and teachers alike. Held on a bright and sunny day, the school grounds transformed into a vibrant fairground filled with colorful decorations, games, food stalls, and music. From the moment the gates opened, the air buzzed with laughter and energy. There were game stalls offering everything from ring toss and balloon darts to lucky draws and spin-the-wheel challenges. Children moved from stall to stall, collecting prizes and cheering each other on. The bouncy castle and carousel were big hits with the younger crowd, while older students enjoyed face painting, henna, and interactive games.

Sports

The annual sports day at our school is one of the most eagerly awaited events of the year. It is a day filled with excitement, energy, and enthusiasm as students, teachers, and parents come together to celebrate sportsmanship and talent. The day begins with a grand opening ceremony, where the school’s sports teams march in their uniforms, and the principal delivers an inspiring speech. Throughout the day, various athletic events take place, including track and field competitions, relay races, and fun activities like sack races and tug-of-war. Students from different age groups participate in these events, showcasing their physical abilities and team spirit. The event also promotes healthy competition and encourages teamwork, discipline, and perseverance.

Annual party

The annual party at our school is a highlight of the year, bringing together students, teachers, and staff for an evening of fun, laughter, and celebration. This much-anticipated event is filled with exciting activities, music, and delicious food, creating an unforgettable atmosphere. The party typically starts with a welcome speech, followed by performances from students, such as dance numbers, skits, and musical acts, showcasing their talents and creativity. The highlight of the evening is often the awards ceremony, where students are recognized for their achievements in academics, sports, and extracurricular activities. The decorations add to the festive mood, with vibrant colors and themes that change each year.
